![A photo of (L to R) Piers, me and Peter](/images/2003/12/small_cebit_r3linux_38813.jpg)

Ahh, it's always great to be able to attend the world's biggest ICT
(information and communication technology) fair -
[CeBIT](http://www.cebit2004.de). And of course being an SAP hacker, it goes
without saying that a visit to the SAP stand is a must.

I was there way back when, with colleagues and friends - among them my
SAP hacking partner-in-crime Piers, on the historical occasion of SAP's
CeBIT announcement of R/3 on Linux. We were even so geeky as to take a
picture recording the event, under the watchful eye of Tux the Linux
penguin mascot.

Anyway, one big reason to visit Hannover in March is because SAP is
intending to make available a new version of the Linux-based evaluation
WAS system, at release 6.40, including the all-important ABAP stack.
Thanks SAP, especially the [Linux Lab](http://www.sap.com/linux/) folks
and also those at SAP who bore the brunt of my recent emails about
this - you know who you are 😉

Yippee!
