Mike Zaschka, speaker, developer and active member of the community, has [just
announced](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mike-zaschka-7395949_sap-abap-ui5-ugcPost-7510710834222948352-xfEr/)
"onedevatlas", which feels to me like something we need but didn't realise it.

The SAP community has produced various catalogues of tools and libraries in the
past, and these have been great. But perhaps in today's age, where it's easier
than ever to create new software, the need for awareness and knowledge of the
tools and libraries that are being built, and of the people building them, is
greater than ever. Moreover, the way such interlinked data can be maintained
and consumed is perhaps also coalescing in parallel with how graphs of
information are now core components of agentic computing. [RDF](/tags/rdf)
anyone?

I am minded to think about the difference between previous catalogues, and
today's ambitions with onedevatlas, as being similar to how it was between
[Yahoo!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo_Directory) - a predominantly
human-maintained catalogue of resources on the Web - and Google - which
subsequently came along with a sledgehammer of a search engine that did away
with the need for explicitly curated structure.

Seeing how Mike navigates between nodes and across arcs (yes, I did choose
those words specifically) in his demo video, gives us a glimpse at what's
promised. I for one am intrigued.

Mike's asking for feedback, so what are you waiting for?
